Cautum Credit / 2. Motivation

The problem is reconstructing the answer

A specialty healthcare lender needs to know how a reported receivables balance became an illustrative borrowing base, and which assumptions deserve follow-up.

The proposed user is a credit analyst overseeing several provider borrowers. Their review must distinguish billed charges, modeled allowed amounts, collection assumptions, policy reductions and advance rates. A portfolio manager also needs a reproducible comparison between reporting periods.

The current-workflow hypothesis

Spreadsheets and existing asset-based lending systems are the baseline. The pain to investigate is repeated evidence reconstruction: matching a flag to its source rows, applying the correct policy version, and preparing a review note someone else can check. No lender interview has established how frequent or costly this problem is.

A real observation, with a narrow conclusion

Founder Ian Cabansag completed two synthetic tasks correctly. Worksheet: 76.89s. Cautum: 132.53s. The exercise does not support a speed-saving claim. It gives an initial usability observation and a concrete reason to test onboarding and evidence navigation with independent analysts.

No paying customer, lender endorsement, financing volume, measured buyer pain cost or provider benefit is claimed. The business model remains a lender subscription/implementation hypothesis.

What is implemented

The application combines visible policy factors, cohort-level cent reconciliation, exception-to-source navigation, dated synthetic monitoring and a tool-using AI investigator. The investigator can choose a borrower to inspect, but it cannot invent delivered financial wording, change amounts or approve credit.

What is not established

This combination is a product hypothesis, not a proven moat. Switching value depends on data compatibility, credit-officer agreement and a workflow benefit that exceeds the integration and governance burden. Aggregate data may be insufficient for the intended buyer.

Primary sources: Orcho product; Klaim; MedLiquidity. Public company claims are attributed, not endorsed. No comparative accuracy or traction claim is reproduced.

Course requests specify gpt-6-luna through Portkey. Live responses reported gpt-6-luna-global and the gateway reported azure-openai. Each stage uses the full original Decimal chain, rounds each cohort HALF_UP to cents, then aggregates. Intermediate rounded values are not chained. Original inputs, unrounded results, adjustments and policy reasons remain inspectable.

Changing advance from 80% to 70% changes the illustrative base from $761,801.19 to $666,576.03; policy-adjusted collateral remains $952,251.44. These are fictional sensitivity factors, not lender standards.

Collection factors and denial rates may double-count losses. “Policy-adjusted collateral” does not establish legally enforceable collateral. Reserves, debt outstandings and net undrawn availability are unassessed.

The lower-cost configuration reduced source coverage in this synthetic sample. Every admitted ID was validated against inspected tool results. That admission rule does not prove that the selected flags were the most useful, that all material risks were found, or that real credit outcomes improve.

Failures informed the final design

Earlier runs repeated a borrower or exhausted an output cap. The runner rejected them. The final tool schema removes inspected borrowers from its allowed IDs; focused Luna requests use reasoning effort none. Failure records remain in ai-evaluation-before-repair.json and ai-evaluation-output-cap-failures.json.

Spending and latency bounds

32,000-character transcript bound; 16,000-character tool-result bound; one tool call per turn; 30-second per-request timeout within a 60-second review deadline; zero SDK retries; default 25 upstream attempts per server process. Process budgets reset on restart and require external quotas for production.

Earlier gateway cache HITs replayed response IDs and token counts. Cached observations are separated from fresh generation results. Gateway billing and complete account spend remain unverified.

Financial limits

Fictional recovery and policy factors; possible double discounting; no reserves, outstandings, disputes, recoupment, actual cash-remittance reconciliation or legal eligibility determination. The two-period study uses fictional fixtures and cannot establish cash collections or causal aging migration.

Product and commercial limits

No EHR, claims clearinghouse, payer or banking integration. No paying customer, pricing validation, independent lender approval or defensible switching advantage. The founder observation did not show improved speed.

Security and operational limits

Localhost-only, single-user, memory-only. No authenticated policy approvals, tenant controls, production retention/deletion system, signed durable audit, external quotas or incident response operation. Fingerprints are reproducible checksums, not signatures or compliance proof.

The long-term healthcare credit-infrastructure thesis remains conditional on real demand, credible methodology, permissioned evidence and specialist counsel. An attractive demonstration does not satisfy those gates.

python3.12 -m venv .venv
