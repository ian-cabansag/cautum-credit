# Visual and editorial system

## Identity
Cautum = caution, safekeeping, financial discipline; **Cautum Credit** as current business and product. Never imply trademark clearance. Premium lender terminal feels closer to a boutique investment firm or modern private bank than a flashy SaaS AI landing page.

## Promise
**Intelligence, with discipline.** Alternative concise lines: **Every dollar deserves a clearer story.** / **Make collateral explainable.** / **From receivables to conviction.** No promise of guaranteed collections, instant liquidity or risk-free financing.

## Palette
Deep botanical green `#173D33`, pine `#112E29`, bone `#F6F7F4`, cloud white `#FFFFFF`, soft mint `#E9F3ED`, sage `#84A796`, muted amber `#C78E53`, restrained rose `#B56E62`.

## Type and composition
- Editorial serif (Georgia fallback) for high-level headlines, sparing use; sans for all financial data.
- Real hierarchy: one anchor statement per screen, information blocks with 16–24 px negative space, restrained radius 9–16 px.
- Avoid emoji dashboards, gradient everywhere, large animation, excessive badges, parallax, duplicate KPI cards, frosted glass, 3D stock photos, glowing AI sparks on every card.
- Treat financial numbers as sacred: tabular numerals, clear denominators, unit alignment, avoid fake percentages or uncited trend arrows.
- Show `illustrative` near every borrowing-base claim. State analysis vintage/scope; never suggest freshness without dates.

## UX acceptance
A credit analyst can locate: portfolio gross → adjustments → eligible → base, governing policy, breached exceptions, trace, and export, without consulting a tutorial. Mobile should remain functional and accessible. Use real empty states, explicit AI availability, keyboard focus and helpful validation messages.
