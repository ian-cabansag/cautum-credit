# Competitive landscape and differentiated wedge (October 2026)

This is a working hypothesis, not an exhaustive market census. Recheck websites and interview buyers before pitch claims.

| Company | Publicly described focus | Cautum's current wedge / gap |
|---|---|---|
| Orcho | Healthcare A/R/claim context to support lender financing conversations and collateral monitoring | Overlaps materially. Cautum aims for lender-first cross-borrower portfolio surveillance and visible rule-policy exception workflow, but no demonstrated superiority yet. https://www.orcho.ai/product |
| Klaim | Healthcare revenue treasury: forecast, accelerate, recover with data from billing/reimbursement sources | Cautum is not offering acceleration/collections; it starts as lender-side credit and evidence workflow. https://klaim.com/ |
| MedLiquidity | Claims financing and securitization tooling, lender monitoring | Long-range ambition overlaps; Cautum does not currently do any securities or financing. https://www.medliquidity.com/ |
| Traditional spreadsheets and ABL platforms | General borrowing-base certificate tracking, covenant monitoring, policy rules | Cautum's aspiration is healthcare reimbursement-specific normalization and review; pilot requires side-by-side validation to prove any incremental value. |

**Do not claim to be the first provider of AI for healthcare receivables.** Do not describe Orcho/Klaim/MedLiquidity as validated inferior products. Cautum's defensible opportunity may be narrower: better **lender workflows** across providers, evidence-chain-based exceptions, model governance, explainable audit trace, verified feedback loops and lower integration burden. This remains an untested differentiation hypothesis.

## Critical discovery questions
1. Who is already paying for A/R underwriting/servicing software, and why do existing products fail their daily workflow?
2. What does a lender receive from providers today, in what format/frequency, and how much analyst time does normalization take?
3. Are enough lenders willing to pay before claim-level integration? What is minimum credible data granularity?
4. Will lenders provide redacted exception examples and target-policy manuals under NDA?
5. Does the buyer require independent calculation fidelity, covenant monitoring, SOC 2, or direct integration before procurement?
6. Can we reliably estimate risk without expensive protected claims integrations?
