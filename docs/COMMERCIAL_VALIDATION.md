# Prompt 11 · Skeptical buyer review and smallest tests

Date: 2026-10-25. All buyer reactions below are **role-play hypotheses**, not actual interviews, quotes, market findings or lender endorsements. Sources informing overlap: primary competitor pages reviewed in COURSE_EVIDENCE.md. No outreach, offer, contract or commitment was sent.

## Narrow initial ICP hypothesis
A specialty healthcare receivables lender whose analyst already reviews several provider borrowers using monthly aggregate schedules and a documented spreadsheet policy. Buyer: credit/risk or portfolio operations lead; daily operator: analyst; data counterpart: provider finance/RCM team. Begin only with constructed synthetic cases, export-based review and reconciliation. Exclude buyers who require immediate claim-level integrations, transaction execution or production hosting before any workflow observation. Whether enough such buyers exist and can pay remains unknown.

## Proposed design-partner offer (hypothesis, not an executed contract)
One lender, one agreed synthetic portfolio, independently supplied policy workbook, two observed review cycles and an export/evidence handoff. Deliver exact reconciliation and a documented unresolved-exception list. Define data rights, confidentiality, review authority, security scope, acceptance criteria and permitted use before engagement. Exclude live integration, loan decisions, funding, compliance certification and guaranteed ROI. A fixed scoped fee and any subsequent subscription would be negotiated from measured value and actual buyer budget; no price or pipeline is claimed.

Proposed acceptance criteria to agree in writing: identical source/policy inputs; exact cents under an agreed rounding convention; all pre-labeled material exceptions accounted for; reviewer can reconstruct selected exclusions; complete timed comparison including preparation/help/errors; every unsupported output clearly identified. Agreement on criteria does not itself mean success.

## Decision log
Implemented evidence/workflow improvements justify requesting independent synthetic review next. No observed buyer evidence justifies expanding into claim integrations, financing, securities, paid pricing assumptions, public deployment or enterprise procurement claims. Founder ownership and lender-first positioning remain unchanged. Discovery failure or a sufficient incumbent workbook is a valid reason to narrow or abandon the current wedge.
