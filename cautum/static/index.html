How the model works

Gross A/R × (1 − contractual adjustment) × collection factor × (1 − denial rate) × aging factor × payer factor × advance rate

The model assumes separate, independent haircuts. This may over-discount balances if historical collections already capture denial losses. Validate and recalibrate before commercial use.
